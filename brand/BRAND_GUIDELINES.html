About

Private by design

VoiceScribe records, transcribes, summarizes, and answers questions about your conversations using modern AI that runs entirely on your phone — no account, nothing uploaded. It is the privacy-first alternative to cloud transcribers like Otter and Fireflies. That single promise — your voice never leaves your device — drives every brand decision below, starting with the colour.

Archetype — Sage, with a Magician facet

The Sage seeks truth and keeps confidence: we turn messy speech into clear knowledge you own, and we keep it yours. The Magician facet is the quiet delight — speech becomes structured insight, locally, as if by sleight of hand. See STRATEGY.md for the full verbal identity.

Logo

The Aperture Wave

A waveform contained within a rounded aperture: voice (the bars) kept private and on-device (the enclosure). The mark is self-grounding, so it reads on light or on the #080A0F dark base.

Clearspace & minimum size

Keep clear space of at least the height of one waveform bar (¼ of the mark) on all sides. Minimum legible size for the icon mark is 24 px — verified (brand/app_icon/generated/mark_24.png). Do not place the wordmark below 80 px wide.

Misuse

Don't skew, stretch, or rotate.

Don't recolour off-palette.

Colour

Signal Teal

One ownable hue replaces the old blue-vs-indigo split. Privacy = trust (blue) + safe/permission (green); teal is their intersection. It is the optical complement of the reserved recording-red, so the UI tells one calm teal story and red only ever means "recording." 60–30–10: neutral surfaces ≈60%, Signal Teal ≈30%, warm accent + recording-red ≈10%.

Voice

Calm. Precise. Private.

  1. Calm and precise. Short, concrete sentences. No hype.
  2. Privacy-respecting. Name where data lives; reduce anxiety, never manufacture it.
  3. Human, not robotic. Plain language; a confidant, not a chatbot.

Accessibility

Inclusive by default

  • Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA in both themes (table above).
  • Ambient backdrop blobs stay at low alpha (0.08–0.10) so foreground text contrast is never reduced below AA.
  • All looping motion (record pulse, ambient drift, pane switch) honours prefers-reduced-motion / disableAnimations; states stay distinct without movement.
  • Dark mode is the native default; light mode is a first-class peer.
