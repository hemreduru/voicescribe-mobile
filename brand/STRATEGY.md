# VoiceScribe — Brand Strategy

> The verbal identity. Everything visual (colour, logo, motion) derives from the
> positioning below — chiefly the one thing VoiceScribe owns that its cloud
> competitors cannot: **your voice never leaves your device.**

---

## Brand essence

**The private home for your spoken words.**

One line, said another way: *modern voice AI that runs entirely on your device, so
what you say stays yours.*

---

## Mission

To let anyone capture, understand, and revisit their spoken words with
state-of-the-art AI — **without ever surrendering them to someone else's cloud.**

## Vision

A world where voice is a first-class, private medium for thinking and working —
where transcription, summarization, and recall happen on the devices people
already own, under their sole control, for free.

---

## Values

1. **Privacy is the product** *(ownership)* — On-device by default. Your audio,
   transcripts, and summaries never leave your device unless you explicitly choose
   a cloud option. Ownership of your words is the feature, not a setting.
2. **Calm precision** — Accuracy and clarity over hype. A quiet interface that
   respects attention and gets out of the way.
3. **Earned trust through transparency** — We say plainly what runs locally and
   what (optionally) doesn't. No dark patterns, no silent uploads, no account
   required to start.
4. **Capability without compromise** — Cutting-edge models (Whisper, on-device
   LLMs) made to run free and offline — not gated behind a subscription or a
   server you have to trust.

---

## Positioning statement

> For **professionals, students, and anyone who thinks out loud** who need to
> capture and make sense of spoken words but **refuse to hand private
> conversations to a third-party cloud**, **VoiceScribe** is the **on-device voice
> intelligence app** that **transcribes, summarizes, and answers questions about
> your recordings entirely on your own device** — unlike **Otter, Fireflies, and
> other cloud transcribers**, because **your audio is processed locally and never
> leaves your device.**

---

## Archetype — Sage (primary) with a Magician facet

**Sage (primary).** VoiceScribe's core promise is *understanding and truth*:
turning messy speech into clear, trustworthy knowledge you own. The Sage seeks
truth, prizes accuracy, and empowers people with knowledge rather than spectacle —
exactly our calm, precise voice and our respect for the user's intelligence.
Critically, the Sage's bond with the user is **confidential**: the trusted
confidant who keeps what's said in confidence. That maps one-to-one onto the
privacy promise — *we understand your words and we keep them yours.*

**Magician (facet).** The "magic" is real but quiet: speech becomes structured
minutes, searchable memory, and answers — and it happens **locally**, with no
server in sight. The Magician *transforms*; VoiceScribe transforms voice into
insight in the device in your hand. We lean Magician in moments of delight (the
living waveform, the instant on-device summary) but always resolve back to Sage
for trust.

**Why not others.** The differentiator is not self-expression (Creator) or
conquest (Hero) or belonging (Everyman) — it's **private understanding** (Sage)
delivered through a near-magical **on-device** pipeline (Magician). Visually this
justifies a hue that reads as *trust + safety* (teal: blue trust ∩ green
permission) rather than the generic "AI blue/purple," and an identity built on a
**contained** waveform (insight, kept private) rather than a loud broadcast.

## Brand voice

Three principles:

1. **Calm and precise.** Short, concrete sentences. State what happens, plainly.
   No exclamation-driven hype, no breathless adjectives.
2. **Privacy-respecting and reassuring.** Proactively name where data lives ("on
   this device"). Never manufacture anxiety to drive action — *reduce* it.
3. **Human, not robotic.** Plain language over jargon. We are a knowledgeable
   confidant, not a chatbot mascot and not a salesperson.

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Everything stays on this device." | "Military-grade, end-to-end, zero-trust encryption!" |
| "Transcribing locally…" | "AI magic is happening… ✨🤖" |
| "Audio is processed on your device and never uploaded." | "We take your privacy very seriously." (vague) |
| "Tap the mic to start." | "Unleash the power of next-gen voice AI today!!" |
| Name the trade-off when a cloud model is chosen. | Hide that a cloud option uploads audio. |
| "No account needed." | "Sign up to unlock your free trial." |

### Microcopy examples

**Empty state (no recordings yet):**
> **Nothing recorded yet.**
> Tap the mic to start — everything you capture stays on this device.

**Microphone-permission prompt:**
> **VoiceScribe needs your microphone** to transcribe what you say.
> Audio is processed on your device and never uploaded. You can turn this off
> anytime in Settings.
